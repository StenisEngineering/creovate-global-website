FAQ

Common questions.

Do you work with clients in Nigeria?+
Yes — Nigeria is one of our primary markets. We understand the business landscape, connectivity constraints, regulatory environment, and design sensibilities. We've built products specifically for Nigerian SMEs and can invoice in GBP or NGN.
How long does a typical project take?+
A landing page takes 3–7 days. A web application typically takes 3–6 weeks. A full PWA with auth, database, and integrations is usually 4–8 weeks. We give you a clear timeline before any work begins.
What's your payment structure?+
We typically work on a 50% upfront / 50% on delivery basis for smaller projects. For larger builds, milestone-based payments. We accept bank transfer (UK or Nigeria), Wise, and PayPal.
Do you offer ongoing support after delivery?+
Yes. We offer optional monthly retainer support for maintenance, updates, and feature additions. Rates from £80/month depending on scope.
I have an idea but I'm not sure what to build yet. Can you help?+
Absolutely. Product strategy and scoping is something we genuinely enjoy. Send us your idea — we'll help you think through what to build, what order to build it in, and what's realistic for your budget and timeline.
Can I use creovateglobal.com as proof of my business for bank onboarding?+
Yes — our website is a credible business presence that can serve as verification for Tide, Stripe, PayPal, and similar platforms. It shows live products (InvoKit Pro), a physical UK base, a professional contact email, and a clear service offering.
