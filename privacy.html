Legal

Privacy policy.

How we handle the information you share with us. In plain English — no jargon.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Who we are

This website, creovateglobal.com, is operated by Creovate Global Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom (company number 17337012), registered office 61 Bridge Street, Kington, HR5 3DJ, United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email us at hello@creovateglobal.com.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you submit the contact form, we collect:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • the service you're interested in and rough budget;
  • the details of your project or message.

How we use it

We use these details for one purpose: to read and respond to your enquiry. We do not use your information for marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.

How your message reaches us

Your form submission is delivered to our inbox using Resend, an email delivery service that processes the message on our behalf. It travels through our hosting provider, Cloudflare. These providers process the data only to deliver your message to us.

Cookies & analytics

This is a static website and we do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our host, Cloudflare, may set an essential security cookie and collect anonymous, aggregate traffic statistics (such as page views and country) to keep the site secure and running. This does not identify you personally.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to deal with your request and for our normal business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to how we use it;
  • complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

To exercise any of these, just email hello@creovateglobal.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with a revised date above.
