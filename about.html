Creovate Global was built by a founder who has spent years understanding what it actually takes to run a small business — across Nigeria and the UK. From managing a food business to building church transport systems to shipping SaaS tools, the experience is hands-on and every product reflects it.

The studio was founded on a simple belief: most digital tools are built for Silicon Valley, not for the trader in Lagos or the freelancer in Sunderland. Creovate fills that gap with practical, affordable, offline-capable software.

With a background in International Business Management (postgraduate) and hands-on product-building experience across fintech, food commerce, invoicing, and community tools, Creovate operates at the intersection of business thinking and technical execution.

Our story

Built from real experience, not theory.

Creovate didn't start with a pitch deck. It started with a problem: small businesses in Nigeria and the UK were underserved by existing tools — too expensive, too complex, or simply not built for their reality.

The first product, InvoKit Pro, came from watching business owners manage invoices in WhatsApp chats and handwritten receipts. That's the kind of problem Creovate exists to solve.

Over time the scope has grown — food commerce, microfinance operations, community platforms, church systems — but the mission stays the same: practical tools that work in the real world.
